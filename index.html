COOKIE NOTES

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CookieNotes (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information you enter and store

CookieNotes lets you save recipes and personal recipe notes. These items are stored locally on your device using the app’s local storage. The current release is configured with CookieNotes CloudKit sync disabled, and CookieNotes does not require an account to use its core features. Do not assume that information is transmitted to us for storage.

Your device’s own backup or sync settings, if enabled, may separately affect how app data is backed up by Apple.

2. Recipe search and TheMealDB

When you search for recipes online, the app sends the search request to TheMealDB service (themealdb.com) to retrieve recipe results. TheMealDB may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address and request details, and handles that information under its own policies. Please review TheMealDB’s website and privacy information for details about its practices.

Recipes you choose to save are stored locally in CookieNotes, subject to the device backup settings described above.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

CookieNotes displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising technology may collect or receive information from the app or device, which can include device identifiers, ad-related interactions or data, and diagnostic or performance information. This information may be used for ad delivery, frequency capping, reporting, fraud prevention, analytics, and service operation, depending on Google’s SDK behavior, your consent choices, and the app’s configuration.

CookieNotes is intended to request non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads are not based on a user’s past behavior for ad personalization, but this does not mean the ad SDK collects no data or that every form of processing is disabled. Google’s handling of information is governed by Google’s terms and privacy disclosures.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob iOS data disclosure.

4. Tracking and privacy choices

CookieNotes does not require you to create an account. CookieNotes does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not use the device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. Banner ads are requested as non-personalized ads.

Advertising and consent behavior may still depend on your region, the choices you make in any consent flow shown by the app or Google’s User Messaging Platform, iOS privacy settings, and the current AdMob configuration. If a consent message is shown, use it to review the choices offered.

We do not use CookieNotes recipe notes to build an advertising profile. Third-party providers may process data as described in their own policies.

5. Diagnostics and app operation

The advertising SDK may collect crash, performance, or related diagnostic information to help operate, maintain, and improve advertising services. CookieNotes does not intentionally collect this diagnostic information directly for its own analytics service.

6. Data retention and deletion

Recipe data and notes saved in the app remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to any device-level backup or sync behavior. Information processed by TheMealDB or Google is retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and settings. CookieNotes does not control those providers’ independent retention practices.

7. Children’s privacy

CookieNotes is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may apply their own age-related and regional requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or its services change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version was last updated. Material changes will be reflected by publishing an updated policy.
